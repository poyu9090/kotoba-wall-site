隱私權政策

鎖屏背日文（LockVocab - Japanese Words）· 最後更新:2026 年 10 月 · English

「鎖屏背日文」是一款以本機運作為主的日文單字學習工具。我們的原則很簡單:你的學習紀錄只留在你的裝置上;我們不需要帳號、不蒐集能識別你的資料、不追蹤你。

我們蒐集哪些資料?

我們不蒐集能識別你的個人資料。本 App 不要求註冊或登入,不建立帳號。

為了了解功能有沒有人用、哪裡需要改善,我們蒐集匿名的使用統計,例如使用了哪些功能、產生鎖屏桌布的次數、App 版本、裝置型號與國家/地區。這些資料連同 App 安裝時隨機產生的匿名代碼,送到我們架設在 Cloudflare 上的伺服器,只保存代碼的雜湊;不含姓名、Email、聯絡人、照片或位置,也不保存 IP 位址(國家/地區由連線判斷後只留兩碼國家代碼)。這些資料無法連結到你的身分,不會用於廣告或追蹤,最多保留 2 年。

資料儲存

你的學習紀錄(會/不會標記、收藏、自己加的單字、單字進度、桌布樣式設定、選用的背景照片)全部儲存在你 iPhone 的本機,不會上傳。你可透過刪除 App 一併移除。

網路連線

背單字與產生鎖屏桌布都不需要網路。只有以下情況會連網:

  • 傳送上述匿名使用統計(我們的伺服器)。
  • App 內購買與恢復購買,由 Apple 的 App Store 處理。

單字發音使用 iOS 內建的語音合成,在你的裝置上完成。

第三方

  • 匿名使用統計存在我們自己的伺服器,由 Cloudflare 代管。不使用任何廣告或跨 App 追蹤 SDK。
  • 除了上述用途,不出售、也不與其他第三方分享資料。
  • App 內購買(完整版解鎖)由 Apple 的 App Store 處理;相關交易資訊受 Apple 隱私權政策規範,我們不會取得你的付款資訊。

追蹤

本 App 不會跨 App 或跨網站追蹤你的行為,也不使用廣告識別碼(IDFA)。

兒童

本 App 適合所有年齡使用,不會向任何使用者(包含兒童)蒐集個人資料。

政策變更

若本政策有更新,我們會修改本頁內容並更新上方日期。

Privacy Policy

LockVocab - Japanese Words · Last updated: October 2026

LockVocab - Japanese Words is a Japanese vocabulary app that works mostly on your device. Your study data stays on your device. No account is needed, we do not collect data that identifies you, and we do not track you.

What we collect

To learn which features are used and what to improve, the app sends anonymous usage statistics, such as which features were used, how many wallpapers were generated, the app version, the device model and the country/region. They are sent with a random anonymous code created when the app is installed to our own server hosted on Cloudflare, which stores only a hash of that code. They contain no name, email, contacts, photos or location; IP addresses are not stored (the country is derived from the connection and kept as a two-letter code only). This data cannot be linked to you, is never used for advertising or tracking, and is kept for at most 2 years.

Data on your device

Your study progress, favorites, words you add, wallpaper settings and any background photo you choose are stored only on your iPhone and are never uploaded. Deleting the app removes them. Pronunciation uses iOS's built-in speech synthesis on your device.

Third parties

  • Usage statistics are stored on our own server, hosted by Cloudflare. No advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs are used.
  • We do not sell or share data with any other third party.
  • In-app purchases are handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy; we never receive your payment details.

Tracking and children

The app does not track you across apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). It is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
